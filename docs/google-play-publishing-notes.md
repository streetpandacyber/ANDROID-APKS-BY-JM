# Google Play Publishing and Monetization Notes

## Expo EAS Submit

Expo's official Android submission guide states that a Google Play Developer account and a Play Console app are prerequisites. EAS Submit requires a Google Service Account key uploaded to the EAS project's Android service credentials. New apps should be submitted as Android App Bundles (`.aab`) to a testing track; the current project preview profile produces an APK for device testing, so a separate production AAB profile is required for Play submission.

Source: https://docs.expo.dev/submit/android/

## Google Play payouts

Google Play's official payout guidance states that orders processed, refunded, or charged back during a calendar month are generally paid around the 15th of the following month, subject to banking delays and the applicable minimum balance. Payout settings are managed in Play Console under Settings > Payments profile. Available payout method and minimum depend on the merchant location and currency.

Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/137997?hl=en
